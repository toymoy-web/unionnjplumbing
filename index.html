About Union Apex Plumbing

A Local Plumber, Not A Call Center

Union Apex Plumbing is based in Union Township, in Union County, New Jersey, an inner-ring suburb of Newark with a housing stock that spans pre-war construction near the township's older sections to newer builds further out. That mix means we see everything from century-old cast iron drain lines to modern PEX plumbing on the same day, and we come prepared for both.

New Jersey plumbers are licensed through a statewide system managed by the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, a different structure than the municipal-only licensing used in some other states. Every technician on our team works under that licensing standard, and every job gets a real, upfront price before any work begins, whether it's an emergency call at 2am or a scheduled water heater replacement booked a week out.

Union County is one of the densest counties in the country, with essentially no unincorporated land left, which shapes some of what we see day to day: aging cast iron and galvanized plumbing in older housing, real winter freeze risk between December and March, and basement flood exposure tied to the Rahway and Elizabeth River watersheds during heavy Mid-Atlantic storms. Knowing that local context is part of diagnosing a problem correctly the first time, not just showing up with a generic toolkit.

We're not a national franchise routing your call through a distant dispatch center. Calls to our line are answered locally, and the technician who shows up is familiar with the specific plumbing quirks of this part of Union County, from the housing eras common in each town we serve to which local landmarks and watersheds shape real, checkable risk factors like winter freezing and storm drainage.
