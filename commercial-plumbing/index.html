Commercial Plumbing in Union, NJ

Restaurants, retail, offices, and multi-unit properties across Union Township and Union County rely on plumbing that can't afford extended downtime.

Commercial plumbing calls differ from residential ones in a few consistent ways: higher fixture counts, code requirements specific to the business type, and a much lower tolerance for downtime, since a closed restroom or a backed-up kitchen drain has a direct impact on business operations. We work with property managers, business owners, and commercial tenants across Union Township and the surrounding Union County business corridors along Route 22 and near the Kean University area.

Commercial leak detection specifically is a real gap area industry-wide, with a meaningful share of commercial leak calls going unresolved on the first visit due to access or fulfillment issues. We prioritize getting it right the first time, including camera and acoustic diagnostics before recommending any repair.

Common Commercial Services

What We Handle

Grease Trap Service

Pumping, cleaning, and repair for restaurant and food service grease traps, a code-required maintenance item.

Commercial Leak Detection

Locating leaks in multi-unit properties and larger commercial systems without unnecessary demolition.

Restroom & Fixture Maintenance

High-traffic commercial restrooms need faster response than residential, given the business impact of downtime.

Backflow Testing

Many commercial properties have annual backflow testing requirements tied to their specific equipment and connections.

Working With Property Managers

Coordinating Access And Scheduling

A real share of our commercial calls come through property managers overseeing multiple tenants or units, sometimes on behalf of a tenant who reported the issue rather than the ultimate decision-maker on the repair. We coordinate directly with property management for access, approval, and documentation, and can work around business hours when a closure would be disruptive.

Code Compliance

Why This Matters More For Businesses

Commercial properties face additional plumbing code requirements beyond residential, including backflow prevention, grease trap sizing for food service, and accessibility requirements for restroom fixtures. We work within these requirements rather than treating a commercial job like a larger residential one.

Multi-Tenant Buildings

A Different Kind of Coordination Problem

Commercial properties along the Route 22 corridor and near the Kean University area often house several tenants under one roof, sharing plumbing infrastructure that a single tenant's complaint doesn't fully explain on its own. A restroom backup reported by one tenant can trace back to a shared stack serving several units, meaning the fix and the responsible party aren't always the same as whoever placed the call. We diagnose the actual source before assuming the reporting tenant's unit is where the problem originates, and we document findings clearly enough for property management to allocate cost and responsibility correctly.

Preventive Maintenance for Businesses

Why Reactive-Only Service Costs More

Commercial properties that only call when something has already failed tend to see more emergency, after-hours calls over time than those on a scheduled preventive maintenance plan. Grease trap pumping on a fixed schedule, periodic camera inspection of aging sewer laterals serving the property, and routine fixture checks in high-traffic restrooms all catch developing problems before they become a closed restroom or a flooded kitchen during business hours. For a property with several years of deferred maintenance, we can walk the full building once and prioritize a punch list rather than addressing everything reactively one emergency at a time.
