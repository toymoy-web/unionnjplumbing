Shower & Bathtub Installation in Union, NJ

Replacing an old tub or shower, or converting one to the other, involves plumbing work beyond just setting the new fixture in place.

A shower or bathtub replacement in an older Union Township bathroom often uncovers plumbing behind the fixture that hasn't been touched since the original construction: aging shutoff valves, a drain configuration that needs adjustment for the new unit, or supply lines that make sense to update while the wall is already open. We handle the full scope, not just setting the new fixture, so the finished installation doesn't reveal a hidden plumbing issue a few months later.

Tub-to-shower conversions are a common request, particularly from homeowners planning to age in place or simply preferring a shower-only bathroom, and we handle the plumbing reconfiguration that conversion requires.

Installation Types

Matching The Project To The Bathroom

Standard Tub or Shower Replacement

Direct swap in the same footprint, the most common and straightforward project.

Tub-to-Shower Conversion

Removing a tub and reconfiguring drain and supply lines for a shower-only installation.

Walk-In / Accessible Installation

Lower-threshold or walk-in tub and shower options for accessibility needs.

Valve and Fixture Upgrades

Updating aging shower valves to modern pressure-balanced or thermostatic models during the same visit.

What's Behind The Wall

Why We Check Before Installing

Older Union Township bathrooms sometimes have shower valves and supply lines that predate modern anti-scald pressure-balancing requirements. When we open the wall for a new installation, we check whether the existing valve meets current code and recommend an upgrade where it doesn't, since it's a meaningfully easier fix while the wall is already open than as a separate future project.

Drain Configuration

A Common Complication

Bathtub and shower drains sit at different heights and positions, which means a tub-to-shower conversion, or even swapping between two different tub models, sometimes requires drain relocation work under the floor. We assess this during the estimate rather than discovering it mid-installation.

Waterproofing Behind the Wall

Why This Step Gets Skipped

A shower or tub installation is only as good as the waterproofing behind the visible surface, and this is a step that's sometimes shortcut on lower-quality installations since it isn't visible once the job is finished. We install proper waterproof membrane and correctly slope the subfloor toward the drain before any tile or surround goes in, since water finding its way behind an improperly waterproofed wall is one of the more expensive problems to discover months or years after installation, often requiring the entire enclosure to be removed and redone.

Fixture and Surround Options

What We Install

We work with acrylic and fiberglass one-piece surrounds, tile-ready installations for a custom finished look, and prefabricated shower base and wall panel systems, matching the option to both budget and the homeowner's preference for maintenance versus custom appearance. One-piece surrounds are typically faster to install and easier to keep clean, while tile allows more design flexibility at a higher labor cost.
