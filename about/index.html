About Union Apex Plumbing

A Union Township based plumbing company serving Union County with licensed repair, installation, and emergency service.

Union Apex Plumbing is based in Union Township, New Jersey, and serves Union County along with nearby communities in our standard dispatch radius. We handle everything from routine fixture repair to full water heater replacement and 24/7 emergency response for burst pipes, no-hot-water calls, and active leaks.

New Jersey runs plumbing licensing through a statewide system managed by the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, under the Division of Consumer Affairs. A Master Plumber license requires passing trade, business and law, and practical exams, a genuinely different structure than the municipal-only licensing systems used in some other states. All plumbing work is performed to New Jersey's Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode, based on the National Standard Plumbing Code (NSPC), the standard the state's Department of Community Affairs adopts and amends rather than the IPC or UPC used in many other states.

Union Township itself has a real history worth knowing if you're new to the area: settled in 1749 as Connecticut Farms and incorporated as its own municipality in 1808. It was later the site of the Battle of Connecticut Farms on June 7, 1780, part of the same British campaign that included the Battle of Springfield days later. Kean University, founded in 1855 and relocated to Union in 1958, anchors the township with close to 13,900 students. That long municipal history is directly relevant to what we see on service calls. A township this old carries genuine pre-war and mid-century housing stock, meaning original cast iron drain lines, galvanized supply pipe, and outdated fixture rough-ins are still common here in a way they simply aren't in a newer suburb. It's exactly the kind of infrastructure we're set up to diagnose correctly on the first visit. We work throughout the township and the surrounding Union County communities that share this same dense, inner-ring-suburb housing character.

Every job starts with a real diagnosis and an upfront price before any work begins. We tell customers plainly when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, and the reverse, rather than defaulting to whichever is more profitable.
