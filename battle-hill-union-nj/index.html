The Area

Where Battle Hill Is

Battle Hill is the area of west Union served by Battle Hill Elementary School. It is bordered by the Rahway River on the west, Morris Avenue on the north, the west branch of the Elizabeth River on the east, and Route 22 on the south. Battle Hill Terrace is one of the streets that carries the name.

Battle Hill is residential, and a tract-level housing summary for the Battle Hill East area describes mostly owner-occupied homes with many built between 1940 and 1969. That is a general picture of an area, not a statement about any one house, so we treat each property on its own merits.

Water And Basements

Why Drainage Comes Up Here

With a river along one side and a river branch along another, groundwater and drainage are fair topics for Battle Hill homeowners. We are not claiming any particular street floods or that basements here are more prone to problems. This is general guidance for any home near waterways.

A working sump pump is the first line of defense for a basement that takes on water. Test yours by pouring a bucket of water into the pit and confirming the pump starts, empties it and shuts off. Check that the discharge pipe sends water well away from the foundation, and consider a battery backup if power outages are a concern.

If a pump runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, or makes unusual noise, it is worth a service call before the next heavy rain rather than after.

Everyday Plumbing

Other Calls From West Union

Beyond sump pumps, the calls we hear from this part of Union are the familiar ones. Water heaters that have been running for many years, drains that slow down over time, and toilets that run or flush weakly are all common. A short visit to diagnose them is usually enough to tell whether repair or replacement makes sense.
